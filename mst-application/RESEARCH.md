## MSt in Real Estate (Department of Land Economy)
- Two years, part-time. Set up in 2016 and led by the **Cambridge Real Estate Research Centre (CRERC)**.
- Aimed at "experienced professionals and those identified as **future leaders** in the real estate industry". It combines academic rigour with significant industry input.
- Teaching mixes distance learning on the VLE with **seven residential weeks in five blocks**. Year 1 has two blocks of two weeks and one of one week. Year 2 has two one-week blocks. There is a dissertation.
- Covers theoretical and practical finance, investment and law applied to **global** commercial real estate markets.
- Entry: first or good 2:1, and normally at least three years of relevant professional experience (Kristiyan has had roughly 2.5 years since February 2024, plus earlier roles to be confirmed).
- Applications go through the Applicant Portal: CV, personal statement ("statement of interest"), references and supporting documents. Everything must arrive by the closing date, and the course can close early once full.
- Bursaries: up to two competitive bursaries (80% and 50% of fees) for applicants who can show academic and professional achievement. **Worth applying for.**
- The latest prospectus found covers 2025–27: landecon.cam.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2024-09/mst_re_2025-27_prospectus_1.pdf

## Jesus College
- **MSt Real Estate students are made members of Jesus College by default**, unless they already have a link to another college. So "why Jesus" is about showing fit, not choosing between colleges.
- Founded in 1496. A large, historic college with a strong welfare reputation, known as a **sporty college**, with football, rugby and cricket pitches on site.
- **Intellectual Forum**: based in West Court. Its Universal Ownership work brought large pension funds together with Cambridge academics on systemic risk for long-term investors.
- **Real estate endowment**: significant residential and commercial holdings. An annual report gave a property return of 11.1% within 15.4% for the whole endowment. The college built **51 Hills Road**, a BREEAM Excellent office in central Cambridge that won nine awards. It sold an industrial property in Leeds (a link to Kristiyan's Leeds background).
- The college has an ambitious climate change plan, and the MCR runs sustainability activity.

## Blues and Varsity football
- Postgraduates on a further degree, including an MSt, can make a limited number of postgraduate appearances.
- **Part-time students are exempt from residency requirements**, but they are expected to take an active part in the sport before selection. Their student status must be "active" for the whole term in which the Varsity match falls.
- The idea is plausible but has to fit the residential structure. Frame it as an aim, not a promise.

## Cambridge personal statement guidance
- Cover relevant skills, experience, academic achievements and motivation. Personal circumstances that affected performance belong here too.
- Research the course and let it shape the statement. Use a logical structure with short paragraphs.
- Open with a specific personal motivation, not "I have always been passionate…". Avoid generic self-description such as "I am hardworking".
- Cover why this course, why this university, career goals, and how the degree gets you there.

## Employer funding: UK tax treatment (to confirm with Hay Wain's tax adviser)
- Employee: work-related training paid for by the employer is exempt from income tax and NIC (ITEPA 2003 ss.250–260) if the course is relevant to current or likely future duties. An MSt in Real Estate for a real estate investment analyst should qualify.
- Employer: the cost is normally deductible for corporation tax. A trading company deducts it as a trade expense; an investment or holding company would usually claim it as a management expense. At 25% corporation tax, the net cost is about 75% of the fees.
- VAT: university fees are exempt, so there is no input VAT to recover either way.
- If Kristiyan pays the fees himself, there is no personal tax relief. An employer-paid route is tax-efficient for both parties.
- A clawback or training-agreement clause, with pro-rata repayment if he leaves within X months, is market standard and does not affect the exemption.
