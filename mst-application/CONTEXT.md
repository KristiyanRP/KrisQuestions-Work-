## Template requirements (research proposal)

500–1,000 words. The template favours research that "builds evidence for action through the development/refinement of frameworks, tools and/or positions/points of view to enable positive impact". Required sections, in this order:

1. Your name
2. Proposed title
3. One-paragraph synopsis
4. Relevance of topic to the course themes (the course-themes attachment has not been provided yet)
5. Main research question (focused, not overly broad)
6. Research context (what is already answered, where the gaps are, with references)
7. Brief overview of research design and methods
8. Likely data and means of access to sources
9. Reference list / bibliography

## Summary of current drafts

**Research proposal: "Strategic Roles of Landlords in UK Retail Turnaround Investments"**
- Topic: how landlords' behaviour in CVAs and Part 26A restructuring plans affects turnaround investors' returns and value creation. The aim is a practical framework for landlord–investor engagement.
- Main research question: how landlords' strategic decisions influence UK retail turnaround success, and which engagement strategies maximise value creation and recovery. Four sub-questions: landlord characteristics, CVA vs restructuring plan, deal structures that align interests, and post-restructuring relationships and exit value.
- Cases cited: Debenhams, Arcadia, House of Fraser, Virgin Active, Prezzo. Statistics cited: PwC's "51% of retail CVAs failed" and a "93% compromise rate" among landlords.
- Methods, in four phases: (1) quantitative analysis of a proprietary family-office database plus public data; (2) case studies; (3) 10–12 semi-structured interviews through professional networks and the BPF; (4) framework synthesis. Includes ethics approval and anonymisation.
- Data: internal deal files through a research agreement; Companies House, BAILII and Westlaw; Insolvency Service; CBRE, Savills and JLL reports.

## Issues found on first read (for later)

1. **Experience length:** the statement says "over five years across European and UK CRE". The resume shows about 2.5 years (from February 2024). Either there is earlier experience not on the resume, or the claim needs to change.
2. **Retail link:** the statement and proposal rely on Modella's retail and turnaround data. The resume's Hay Wain/Modella bullets cover office, industrial, hospitality and residential, not retail turnarounds. The resume or CV should show the retail-restructuring exposure that the proposal depends on.
3. **Academic background:** the statement says Banking and Finance gave a foundation in "urban systems". The real, stronger fact is the residential real estate dissertation, which also fills the placeholder.
4. **Leadership:** the "[insert leadership role]" placeholder can be filled with the football captaincies (professional and university).
5. **Proposal structure:** it should be re-sectioned to match the template headings and include a "relevance to course themes" section. The course-themes attachment is still needed.
6. **Sources:** the proposal's statistics (51% CVA failure, 93% compromise) need exact citations. The Journal of Property Research entry has no author. Some entries are grouped placeholders ("CBRE, JLL and Savills (2020–2025)").
7. **Scope:** the main research question is broad (strategic decisions plus engagement strategies plus recovery), and the template asks for a focused one. There are four sub-questions and a four-phase design, which is ambitious for an MSt dissertation.
8. **Proposal–statement link:** the statement frames the research around "high street restructuring / urban resilience". The proposal frames it around investor returns. The two should tell one consistent story.
9. **Faculty and modules:** check that the named faculty and the "Real Estate Finance module" match the current course.
10. **Research agreement:** the family office in the proposal is presumably Hay Wain/Modella. Access through a formal research agreement should be confirmed with the employer.
